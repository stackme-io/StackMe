Of all fragile findings, ~90% are two patterns: locating by position (.nth/:nth-child) and by loose text (getByText(/…/)). Everything else ≈ 10% (XPath and deep-CSS together ≈ 7%). In 83-94% of projects that have any fragility, one of those two is the dominant cause.

Method

Static analysis of test source - no tests are run. Projects with a live Playwright/Cypress setup, ≥10 locators, not a fork/archive, pushed within 3 years, in three star tiers. Excluded: unit tests (React Testing Library isn’t e2e locators), framework scaffolds, files copied verbatim across repos, and hand-curated non-product repos (courses, bootcamp projects, demos, -example/-starter templates).

Data collected on 7 September 2026. The list of 547 repos was itself selected from GitHub search results. Of those, 296 were analyzed and 251 skipped: 124 with fewer than 10 locators, 60 hand-curated non-product repos, 50 with no test files left to analyze after our filters (including Immich and PostHog), 11 that failed to clone (including n8n, Kibana, Gatsby, Twenty and Element Web) and 6 with an unknown framework. Numbers include one later classifier correction: 40 findings that counted an index inside an attribute value as a position were reclassified from the saved source lines.

Four classes: stable (role/testid/label/hand-written id), fragile (position, :nth-child, loose text, generated classes, deep CSS chains, xpath axes), context (can’t tell from the string - exact text, a plain class, an unknown data-*; not counted as fragility), and dynamic (built at runtime).

Limitations

  • “Fragile” is a static smell, not proven breakage - no flaky/git-history correlation yet.
  • Precision is an estimate, not independent validation: ~96% true fragile in a pattern-based review of an earlier top-cohort run, ~90%+ in a spot check of about 200 tail findings. The mid cohort was not sampled. One non-blind rater.
  • Percentages are noisy on small suites - headlines lean on the pooled rate and a per-project metric.
  • The classification is our heuristic. Found it wrong on your repo? Tell us.
